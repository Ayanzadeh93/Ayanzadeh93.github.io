Research

My current work at the CORAL Lab centers on accessible indoor navigation: turning a building's floor plan into a spatial knowledge graph that a language model can reason over, so that blind and low-vision travelers get route guidance without per-building infrastructure.

I am the principal investigator of the human-subjects study behind that work (UMBC IRB Protocol #1743, InVLM; expedited, approved May 2025). In parallel, with collaborators at UMBC, I work on vision-language models for early wildfire detection from satellite imagery, and on a multi-task network for segmenting and classifying medical images on imbalanced datasets.

Before UMBC (M.S. 2025), in Istanbul and Izmir, I worked on biomedical image analysis, segmenting and tracking cells in label-free phase-contrast microscopy, and on making deep models efficient: knowledge distillation, quantization for edge devices, and graph representation learning.
