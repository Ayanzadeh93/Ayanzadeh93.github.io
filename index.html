About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UMBC (M.S. 2025). I work on computer vision, medical image analysis, and multimodal learning — with a focus on AI that is useful in clinics, the field, and assistive technology.

That includes navigation systems for people with blindness or low vision, vision-language models for wildfire detection, and medical image segmentation under real data constraints.
