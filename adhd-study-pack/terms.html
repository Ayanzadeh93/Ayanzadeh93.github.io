Terms of use

A short agreement for using this focus workspace. Last updated 20 September 2026.

What this is

The ADHD Study Pack is a browser workspace for planning and timing study. It is provided as-is, free, for personal use. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment for ADHD or any other condition.

Your account

You may use it signed out (data stays in this browser), with a local profile on this device, or by signing in with Google. Google sign-in only asks for your name and email so the workspace can follow you to another device. You are responsible for the Google account you use.

Your data

How storage works is described in the Privacy page. You can export or wipe a workspace from Setup. Do not put secrets, other people’s private records, or illegal content in the pack.

Google Calendar

Connecting Calendar is optional. The first connect only asks to view your events. Writing events (send my blocks) is a separate permission you can refuse. You can disconnect at any time. Events the pack created in Google stay there until you remove them from Plan or from Google Calendar.

Availability

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no uptime promise. Features, including Google sign-in and Calendar, depend on Google’s services and may change if those services change.

Acceptable use

  • Do not try to access another person’s workspace
  • Do not abuse Firebase, Google, or this site (scraping at volume, automated account creation, attacks)
  • Do not present the pack as a clinical product

Liability

The pack is offered without warranty. The operator is not liable for lost study data, missed deadlines, or how you interpret the statistics. Keep your own backups via Export in Setup if the work matters.
