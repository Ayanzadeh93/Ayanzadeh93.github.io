# ADHD Study Pack field recordings

These locally hosted MP3 files are derived from public-domain recordings on
Wikimedia Commons. They were transcoded from the original Ogg files at a lower
bitrate for reliable browser playback. Short fades were added at the ends to
make looping less abrupt; `forest.mp3` was shortened to 90 seconds.
